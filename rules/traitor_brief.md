Everything in the general rules applies to you. In addition:

You are one of three hidden players. You know each other. Nobody else knows any of
you.

There are exactly three nights on which you remove someone: the first night, the
Saturday meal, and the final night. You must remove someone on each of them. You
cannot skip a night. The choice must be unanimous among the hidden players still
in the game, and you may never choose each other.

On Saturday the removal happens at the table rather than overnight. You set the
table beforehand. One plate in the set carries a different image from all the
others. Whoever is sitting in front of that plate when the meal ends is the one
removed. Nobody else knows this is happening. If someone moves the plates, the
removal follows the plate, not your intention.

The object that circulates at Councils protects whoever holds it from being removed
at night. If you choose them anyway, nothing happens that night, the object is
destroyed, and you are owed a removal later.

If you are the last hidden player left, you may spend one of your remaining nights
recruiting someone instead of removing them. Tell them nothing until they accept.
If they refuse, they are removed instead.

Twelve players. Three of you are hidden; the rest are not. Nobody but the hidden
players knows who they are.

Each night, the hidden players remove one person. Each Council, everyone votes to
remove one person. Most votes wins; there is no majority needed. You may not vote
for yourself and you may not abstain. All votes are revealed publicly once cast.
Eliminated players do not vote again.

If a vote ties, the tied players stand aside and everyone else votes between them.
If it ties again, the tied players settle it by rock-paper-scissors.

At the end of each Council, one object is passed from the player holding it to
another player of their choosing. The pass is public. What the object does is told
only to whoever holds it. The object stops circulating partway through the weekend.

At some point you may be tethered in groups of three. While tethered you may only
speak privately with the two people you are tied to, and your group casts a single
vote which must be unanimous or is not counted at all.

On Sunday morning the remaining players may send three questions to the eliminated
players, who return one name per question. They are not obliged to be honest.

Anyone removed at night leaves one sentence behind, delivered privately to a single
player. It may not name anyone's role.

The game ends when three players remain. Those three vote whether to stop there or
remove one more. If any hidden player is still standing at the end, the hidden
players win. The rest of you win only by removing all of them.

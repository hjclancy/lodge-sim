Narrative

The twelve seats split three Traitors among nine Faithful, and the split falls in a way that quietly decided the whole game before anyone noticed a pattern. P02 (A03, the low-profile blender), P06 (A10, the passive consensus-follower) and P10 (A08, the cautious deflector) all drew Traitor, and all three archetypes are built around exactly the behavior a hidden player wants: stay quiet, echo the room, never be the one with the strong opinion. Meanwhile every loud, accusatory, or contrarian persona in the mix — P03's aggressive accuser (A02), P08's suspicious pusher (A07), P01's contrarian (A11), P09's impulsive disruptor (A05) — landed on Faithful. The effect over the whole game is that the people built to generate noise and provoke tells were never the ones with anything to hide, while the people built to disappear into the furniture were the actual threat, and the furniture-people never once had to break character to survive.

What this particular playthrough illustrates, more than anything, is how much the ruleset rewards a Traitor trio drawn from quiet, consensus-shaped personas: none of them ever had to lie outright, only to keep nodding along, and the game's noisiest, most suspicious-sounding archetypes turned out to be exactly the innocent people they claimed not to be.
